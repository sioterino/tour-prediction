| artist            | `string`                                                                                                  | performing artist/group's name.                                                                                                                                                                 | identification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

| day_n             | `date`                                                                                                    | date of the last show night at a certain venue                                                                                                                                                  | k-pop consumption and interest rate varies overtime, that, plus the state of economy, can show a trend of more or less successful touring years (e.g. `2023`, or `2026`); seasonality also may affect public, as people are more prone to attend shows during warmer seasons |
| weekday_n         | `('Mon', 'Tue', 'Wed', 'Thu', 'Fri', 'Sat', 'Sun')`                                                       | what day of the week the last show night was                                                                                                                                                    | ticketing goes smoother for weekend shows (`Fri`, `Sat`, `Sun`), as opposed to midweek dates                                                                                                                                                                                 |

| country           | `string`                                                                                                  | country's name (normalized)                                                                                                                                                                     | some countries are harder to sell out (e.g. `mexico`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| city              | `string`                                                                                                  | city's name (normalized)                                                                                                                                                                        | some cities are harder to sell out (e.g. `miami`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| venue             | `string`                                                                                                  | venue's name (normalized)                                                                                                                                                                       | venue's may present more than one name, identifying them correctly is important                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| venue_type        | `('stadium', 'arena', 'concert hall', 'theater', 'amphitheater')`                                         | venue's status and capacity                                                                                                                                                                     | type of venue may carry prestige, selling out a stadium, even though it has a `20k` capacity creates headlines                                                                                                                                                               |

| attendance        | `integer`                                                                                                 | amount of tickets that were purchased                                                                                                                                                           | total attendance across all `show nights` in a venue                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| fill_rate         | `float`                                                                                                   | `attendance / net_capacity`, the percentage of what was sold from what was available                                                                                                            | `sold out` status shows here, depending on the venue a `0.90~0.99` fill rate is considered a technical `sold out` despite not selling out completely                                                                                                                         |
| box_score         | `float`                                                                                                   | gross *revenue* made from ticketing                                                                                                                                                             | safe to assume that every `10,000` tickets an artist gross about `$ 1M` in usd                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| avg_ticket_price  | `float`                                                                                                   | `box_score / attendance`, average ticket price                                                                                                                                                  | reflects demand, but also economy; `macau`, `hong kong`, `singapore` and `taiwan` are some of the countries where tickets are the most expensive, whilst expensive tickets in the `us` reflect higher demand due to *ticketmaster*'s dynamic pricing                         |

| cancelling_reason | `('COVID-19', 'Health', 'Logistical', 'Natural Phenomena', 'Poor Ticketing', 'Unforeseen Circumstances')` | if reported show was cancelled, the reasoning presented by the company's notice                                                                                                                 | contextual                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
